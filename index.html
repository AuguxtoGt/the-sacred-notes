Daily Verse . Verse . Reference . Notes

One Bible verse a day, nothing added

The Today screen shows a single verse, text and reference only, from a public-domain translation: King James Version in English, Louis Segond 1910 in French, and Reina-Valera 1909 in Spanish. There is no commentary and no interpretation: what you think about it is yours to write.

  • Text and reference only
  • Public-domain translations (KJV, LSG 1910, RV 1909)
  • Shown in the language you choose
  • A blank space right below for your notes

Your Journal . Write . Tag . Save

A blank page and a simple question

Below the verse there is one simple question, "What do you want to write down today?", and a blank page. Write a prayer, a note, an intention, or a list of things you are grateful for. What you write is always up to you.

  • As many entries per day as you want
  • Tags you create yourself (family, gratitude, work)
  • Optional reflection mood for each entry
  • Edit or delete any entry later

History & Journey . Search . Calendar . Stats

Find anything you wrote and see your journey

Every entry goes to your History, as a list or on a monthly calendar. Search for a word, filter by tag, date, or favorites. The Journey screen shows your streak, best streak, words written, and a 12-week activity grid.

  • Full-text search, filters, and favorites
  • Monthly calendar of the days you wrote
  • Streaks, activity grid, and reflection summary
  • Export everything as a .txt file

  • Backup and restore with one file
  • Light and dark theme, adjustable font size
  • Built-in customer support tab inside the app
